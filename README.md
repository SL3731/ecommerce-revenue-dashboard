# Ecommerce Revenue Dashboard (Power BI)

## Project Overview
Interactive Power BI dashboard project focused on ecommerce revenue performance, operational efficiency, customer trends, and commercial decision support.

This project demonstrates end-to-end business intelligence workflow including:
- Data cleaning and transformation
- KPI tracking
- Dashboard development
- Revenue and margin analysis
- Business performance storytelling
- Visual analytics for decision-making

---

## Objectives
- Monitor ecommerce sales and profitability performance
- Identify high-performing products and categories
- Analyse customer purchasing behaviour
- Track operational and commercial KPIs
- Support data-driven business decisions

---

## Tools & Technologies
- Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Excel
- Data Modelling
- Business Intelligence Reporting

---

## Key Features
- Revenue and profit trend analysis
- Product/category performance tracking
- Customer segmentation insights
- KPI dashboards and operational monitoring
- Interactive filtering and drill-down capability
