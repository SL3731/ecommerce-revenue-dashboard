# Business Insights Summary

## Key Findings

### Revenue Concentration
A small number of product categories contributed disproportionately to total revenue, suggesting opportunities for targeted inventory planning and promotional optimization.

### Customer Behaviour
Repeat purchasing behaviour showed stronger contribution to overall profitability compared to one-time transactions, indicating the importance of retention-focused strategies.

### Margin Variability
Certain products generated strong topline sales but lower profit margins, highlighting the need to balance revenue growth with margin optimization.

### Operational Visibility
Dashboard monitoring improved visibility into sales trends, product performance, and customer behaviour, enabling faster and more structured decision-making.

### KPI Monitoring
The dashboard was structured to support monitoring of:
- Revenue trends
- Profitability
- Customer activity
- Product performance
- Operational efficiency metrics
